FAQ

Why don't you show your face?

I create product-focused content — hands-on demos, screen recordings, macro b-roll, and professional voiceover. No on-camera talent means no scheduling friction, faster turnarounds, and content your editors can cut straight into ads. For briefs that need a human touch, I offer real human voiceover on every piece.

Do you use AI in production?

Some pieces use AI-assisted rendering alongside filmed and screen-recorded content. Anything AI-assisted is disclosed on-creative and labeled per platform rules (TikTok, Meta, YouTube) — your brand never carries that risk. Sample ads here are spec work for fictional brands, marked as such.

How do usage rights work?

Your base rate covers organic posting. Paid-ad usage is licensed by duration (see rates above), whitelisting is priced per month, and everything is written into a simple agreement before production starts.

How fast is delivery?

Standard turnaround is 3–5 days from approved brief; concepts land in 24–48h. Two revision rounds are included. Rush delivery is available for launches and BFCM windows.

How do we start?

Email a one-liner about your product and goal. You'll get hook concepts and a quote within 48 hours.
