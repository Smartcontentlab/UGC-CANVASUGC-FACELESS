## 1. MISSION & POSITIONING

**What this is:** A faceless-first UGC (user-generated content) creator business, brand name **The Faceless Studio**, run by one human operator with an AI agent doing production, applications, and daily job-sourcing. Faceless content — hands/products/screens only, no on-camera face — is a first-class, brand-requested hiring category (38% of new 2025 creator ventures were faceless, up 217% since 2022; 86% of consumers rate faceless content as *more* authentic).

**The system, end to end:** 9-piece portfolio (live webpage) → 4-gate audit (every piece must score ≥85/100 before a brand ever sees it) → multi-platform launch + always-on direct email outreach → agent-drafted applications with one-click human approval → daily automated job scans feeding a tracker.

**Positioning split:**
- **Lead niche (~50%): Tech / apps / AI tools / SaaS.** Best rate-to-competition ratio. Screen-record + voiceover is the *native* faceless format. $150–$1,500/video.
- **Volume niche (~30%): Beauty / skincare.** Highest job flow in the market; faceless via hands-on demos, macro/texture shots, flat-lays. $75–$400/video.
- **Bridge niche (~20%): Supplements / wellness.** Higher rate ceiling ($100–$500), compliance knowledge is a moat.
- **Third income lane: Canvas UGC** (added 2026-07-16) — running fresh, brand-owned "ambassador" social accounts (not personal, not corporate) at volume for consumer apps/AI/SaaS/fintech. Paid CPM ($2–5/1K views) / CPA / hybrid base+bonus. Preferred structure: hybrid base + performance, $1,500–$3,000/mo retainer for 15–20 posts, or high-volume ($5K–15K/mo at scale). Claims-heavy categories (supplements) excluded from canvas mode. Account ownership/access/payout terms must be in writing.
- **Skip:** food/bev and pets (no expertise/product-access edge).

**Launch pricing (raise after 5 booked jobs):**

## 4. HARD RULES & COMPLIANCE

1. **Nothing sends without approval** except channels explicitly set to full-auto (currently: only discovery scans and drafting into the tracker — actual sends of pitches/applications require user go-ahead, though a blanket "send all now" approval was given 2026-07-21 11:36pm covering a specific batch — treat future batches as needing fresh approval unless the user states otherwise).
2. **No payments.** The agent is not authorized to pay for anything (e.g., ProjectCasting's $19.99/mo premium tier to bypass the 1/day free-application limit, or ThePaidCreator's paid Substack tier to unlock a gated brand name/link) — these are always left as a user decision.
3. **No fabrication.** No fabricated testimonials, no invented brand-usage claims (e.g., the MoneyCoach pitch explicitly states she hasn't used the app yet), no fake social media accounts or follower counts (a subagent violated this once during signup — see Section 9 — and it was caught and partially remediated).
4. **Age-bracket honesty is a hard line.** Real DOB always governs eligibility for age-gated listings; a false age is never used to pass a hard bracket, even when it costs an opportunity (solvely.ai, disqualified 2026-07-22 despite an earlier informal clearance).
5. **AI disclosure rules (non-negotiable):**
   - FTC "reasonable consumer" standard: disclose when AI involvement would change a viewer's belief about authenticity. Disclosure must be clear + conspicuous, same modality as the claim; platform labels alone are not sufficient.
   - TikTok: AI-generated-content toggle at upload for synthetic faces/voices or significant AI edits.
   - Meta: "AI info" label required on paid AND organic AI-created/altered depictions of people.
   - YouTube: "altered or synthetic" declaration at upload.
   - EU AI Act: mandatory disclosure + provenance metadata for synthetic media reaching EU audiences.
   - **Standing production rule:** default mode = real human voiceover over faceless visuals, which sidesteps most AI-disclosure friction entirely. When AI visuals/voice ARE used, disclose at upload and on-creative in plain language. Never pair AI personas with fabricated testimonials or implied credentials.
6. **Fictional spec-brand registry — do not reuse a name for a different product category:** TaskLoop (SaaS planner) · Penny (budgeting app) · Aeris Buds (earbuds) · LUMA Dew Serum (skincare) · RITUAL Skin (skincare line) · GlowPod Pro (LED device). Piece 1 uses REAL tools (Perplexity, Gamma, ElevenLabs) explicitly labeled spec/not-sponsored. Piece 9 (digicam) is 100% real human-shot footage of a real product (Vivitar ClixShot, $15 Target) — zero AI disclosure needed on this piece, a genuine selling point.
7. **Affiliate / #ad rules:** any post carrying an affiliate link must have the line "#ad · commissionable links in bio" placed BEFORE the fold in the caption, plus the platform's AI-disclosure toggle where visuals are AI-assisted. Fictional-brand reuse posts stay labeled spec/concept and are never linked to a real lookalike product. Mix ratio: 8 monetizable / 6 pure-value posts in the seasoning feed — an all-affiliate feed reads spammy and gets suppressed.
8. **No account creation without disclosure/handoff where ToS requires a human** — e.g., ContentCraze (mobile-app-only signup) is queued for user handoff, not agent-automated signup.
9. **Manual-only channels (ToS explicitly bans automation):** Upwork, Fiverr, LinkedIn, Indeed — agent drafts, human pastes and clicks submit.
10. **Compliance audit is built into every piece before it ships** — see Section 6's Gate system.

## 6. PRODUCTION SYSTEM

**Environment quirks (memorize before producing anything):**
- **No `drawtext` ffmpeg filter available** in this sandbox — use ASS/libass subtitle rendering for all burned captions instead.
- **ffmpeg 7.0.2 throws a trailing-packet decode error on ALAC audio** but still decodes the full file — this is non-fatal; crews are warned not to treat it as a failure.
- **Video-generation API's `referenceImages` parameter is non-functional** — use `firstFrameImage` plus separate `GenerateImage` stills for product-faithful renders instead.
- **Video-generation API's `personGeneration` parameter gets rejected** — omit it entirely from calls.
- **Higgsfield MCP** is connected and explored but the account balance (10 credits, free plan) is held as insurance/untouched. Cost preflight via `get_cost:true` on generate calls is free and should always be run before spending.

**Caption standard:** house ASS caption band, burned in, readable muted, hook text on screen by 1.5s. Keyword-first captions (put the searchable product/tool name in the first line of the caption, in on-screen text, and spoken in VO — audio is indexed by TikTok search too; this was validated by the 94.9%-search-traffic digicam video).

**The 4-gate audit system (nothing reaches a brand without passing all 4):**
- **Gate 0 — Brief-fit precheck** (before production): niche match, format match, specs confirmed. Kills wasted production.
- **Gate 1 — Technical QA** (agent, automated checklist): 9:16 ratio, resolution, clean audio mix, captions present, length in spec, hook lands by 1.5s, zero AI artifacts, export quality.
- **Gate 2 — Rubric scoring** (agent, formal): scored against rubric **`cmrn3vm5u03ab06adz0jn7xgm`** ("UGC Portfolio Piece Audit"), 7 weighted criteria totaling 100 — Hook Strength 20, Niche & Brief Fit 15, Direct-Response Structure 15, Faceless Craft & Production Quality 15, Compliance & Disclosure 15, Native Authenticity 10, Brand-Readiness & Specs 10. **Pass gate: ≥85/100.** Below 85 → revise → rescore. Two failed revisions → piece is scrapped and reconceived.
- **Gate 3 — Human final approval:** user reviews and approves every portfolio piece and every application before it's sent, except channels explicitly set to full-auto.
- **Application-level audit (before Gate 3, for pitches):** every brief requirement answered, personalization present, correct niche samples attached, rate matches the card, no compliance red flags.

**Sub-agent crew dispatch pattern (how work actually gets parallelized):**
- **Named production crews** (e.g., Alto/Alto2, Baritone/Baritone2, Chorus/Chorus2) — each assigned a specific batch of portfolio/seasoning pieces for a production task (originally the initial 8-piece build; currently the VO-real-voice swap, split as Alto2=pieces 1-4, Baritone2=pieces 5-9, Chorus2=the 7 kit posts d03-d14).

- **"Quill" / brief-puller subagents** — dispatched to stage a specific single high-value application (e.g., the $3,000 Women's Health brand).
